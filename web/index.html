Before you dig

Your rig, your rules

Use a keyboard, a connected controller or a touch screen. Press Escape to open the pause menu during play.

On a phone or tablet

Steer with your left thumb anywhere on the left of the screen: push up to fly, down to drill. Buttons for what you can use right now appear under your right thumb; drag the bomb or fire button to aim. Tap menu rows directly. Button size and stick side are in Options. On an iPhone, add this page to your Home Screen to play full screen.

Bring a friend

Choose local split-screen in the game menu to play together on this screen. Online multiplayer is not available in the browser yet.

Keep your haul

Save through the pause menu and wait for “Saved in this browser” before closing the tab. Saves stay in this browser on this site's address; clearing site data removes them.

The game is suspended while this help panel is open.
